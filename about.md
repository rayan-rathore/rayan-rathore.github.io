Hi, I'm Rayan.

I'm a self-taught learner currently working my way into programming, cybersecurity, cloud, and eventually AI security.

I'm not coming from a traditional computer science background, and I'm still early in the journey. That's actually one of the reasons I created this website.

I wanted somewhere to document what I'm learning — not just the finished projects, but the mistakes, confusing parts, things that finally clicked, and the things I still don't understand.

Right now, I'm focused on building my foundations in Python and computer science. I'm learning by building projects, breaking things, debugging them, reading documentation, and trying to understand *why* things work rather than simply making them work.

You'll find my projects, notes, experiments, and learning articles here.

I'm particularly interested in where **cybersecurity, cloud computing, and AI** meet. My long-term goal is to become highly skilled in security, with a particular interest in cloud security and AI security.

But I'm trying not to rush toward the title.

There is a lot I need to learn first.

So this website is essentially a record of that process.

I want to be able to look back years from now and see not only what I eventually learned, but where I started, what I struggled with, and how I got there.

If you're also learning something difficult, starting later than you expected, or building your way into tech one project at a time, I hope something here is useful to you.

**I'm learning in public, one project at a time.**
